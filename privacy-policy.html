Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

At Gonah Homes, accessible from https://gonahhomes.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and guests. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and how we safeguard your data, including information processed through our digital applications and integrations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our customers and app users. The types of personal data we may collect include:

  • Accommodation Booking Data: Name, email address, phone number, physical address, billing information, and specific stay preferences or requirements.
  • Google User Data (Google OAuth API): If you choose to log in or connect your Google account to our application, we request access to your primary Google email address and basic profile information.
  • Log Files: Standard web traffic data, such as internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring/exit pages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To process, manage, and confirm your accommodation bookings and payments.
  • To communicate with you regarding your stay, customer service requests, or promotional updates.
  • Google Data Usage: We use your Google account information strictly to authenticate your identity, streamline the login process, and personalize your experience within our platform.
  • To operate, maintain, improve, and optimize our website and user applications.

3. Google API Scopes & Limited Use Policy

Our application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell, rent, or trade your Google user data to third parties. We do not use your Google data for serving advertisements.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We only share your personal data with third parties in the following scenarios:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing your stay (such as secure payment processors), provided they agree to keep this information confidential.
  • Legal Obligations: When required by law, regulation, or legal process to protect the rights, property, or safety of Gonah Homes, our users, or others.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, satisfy your accommodation agreements, or comply with legal, tax, and accounting requirements. Google authentication data is only retained for the duration of your active account session or application authorization.

6. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, update, or request the deletion of your personal information. You can also disconnect our application's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account Security settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
