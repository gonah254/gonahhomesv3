Local Recommendations

Your insider guide to the best of Mombasa — handpicked by our team. From award-winning seafood restaurants to UNESCO heritage sites, make the most of your coastal stay.

Restaurants & Cafés From Swahili street food to fine dining — Mombasa has it all

Tamarind Dhow Restaurant

Mombasa's most iconic dining experience — a floating restaurant on Mombasa Creek serving world-class seafood, lobster, and Swahili cuisine. Book in advance.

Seafood · Fine Dining 📍 Tudor Creek · ~10 min from Nyali
Café Mocha

A Nyali favourite for breakfast, artisan coffee, and light meals. Their fresh juices, avocado toast, and pancakes are crowd pleasers. Free WiFi available.

Café · Breakfast 📍 Nyali Centre · ~5 min drive
Barka Restaurant

Authentic Swahili flavours in the heart of Mombasa Old Town. Try the coconut fish curry, pilau rice, and freshly baked mandazi. A must-try cultural experience.

Swahili · Local Cuisine 📍 Old Town Mombasa · ~15 min
Nyama Choma at Mtwapa

For the full Kenyan grilling experience, head to Mtwapa for freshly roasted nyama choma (grilled meat), ugali, and kachumbari. Buzzing atmosphere especially on weekends.

Kenyan BBQ · Casual 📍 Mtwapa · ~20 min drive
Nakumatt Food Court

A convenient all-in-one shopping and dining destination at City Mall Nyali. International fast food chains alongside Kenyan favourites — great for families.

Food Court · Family 📍 City Mall Nyali · ~8 min
Severin Sea Lodge Bar

Enjoy sundowner cocktails overlooking the Indian Ocean at this beachfront hotel bar. Casual dress code, excellent mocktails, and stunning sunset views.

Beach Bar · Sundowners 📍 Bamburi Beach · ~12 min
Our tip: For the freshest seafood in Mombasa, visit the daily fish market at the Old Port (Mombasa Harbour) in the early morning — vendors sell straight off the boats. Ask us for directions.

Beaches & Water Sports Miles of white sand and warm Indian Ocean waters await

Nyali Beach

The closest beach to most Gonah Homes properties. Clean, calm, and lined with coconut palms. Perfect for morning swims and evening strolls. Water sports available nearby.

Swimming · Walking 📍 Nyali · ~5 min walk/drive
Mombasa Marine National Park

Kenya's oldest marine park — crystal clear waters with vibrant coral reefs, tropical fish, turtles, and dolphins. Snorkeling, glass-bottom boat, and scuba diving offered daily.

Snorkeling · Scuba · Marine Life 📍 Bamburi · ~15 min
Diani Beach

Widely rated among the top beaches in Africa. Pristine white sand, turquoise water, kite surfing, and a vibrant beachfront scene. Worth the 1-hour drive south for a day trip.

Day Trip · Kite Surfing 📍 South Coast · ~1 hr drive
Shanzu Beach

A quieter, less-touristy stretch of North Coast beach. Ideal for peaceful walks, local fishing boat rides, and watching dhow sailboats pass. Ask us to arrange a sunset dhow cruise.

Quiet · Dhow Cruises 📍 North Coast · ~20 min
Water safety: Always swim between the reef flags on public beaches. The Indian Ocean can have strong currents during high tide. Ask our team about the best swimming times each day during your stay.

Attractions & Culture History, wildlife, and the soul of coastal Kenya

Fort Jesus

A UNESCO World Heritage Site built by the Portuguese in 1593. This 400-year-old coastal fort houses a world-class museum documenting Mombasa's Swahili, Arab, and colonial history.

UNESCO · History Museum 📍 Old Town · ~20 min · KSh 1,200 entry
Haller Park (Bamburi Nature Trail)

A remarkable wildlife sanctuary built on a reclaimed quarry. See giraffes, hippos, crocodiles, and giant tortoises up close. Perfect for families with children. Low entry fee.

Wildlife · Family · Nature 📍 Bamburi · ~15 min · KSh 800 entry
Mombasa Old Town

Walk the winding streets of Mombasa's ancient Arab-influenced Old Town. Carved wooden doors, spice markets, Swahili architecture, and the Old Harbour make this an unforgettable half-day excursion.

Culture · Architecture · Heritage 📍 Mombasa Island · ~20 min · Free
Shimba Hills National Reserve

One of the few places in Kenya to spot the rare Sable antelope. The reserve also features lush coastal rainforest, stunning viewpoints, and the scenic Sheldrick Falls — great for a day safari.

Safari · Nature · Day Trip 📍 South Coast · ~1.5 hr drive
Jomo Kenyatta Public Beach

Also known as Pirates Beach — a lively public beach with camel rides, beachside hawkers, traditional dance performances, and a relaxed local vibe. Great for cultural immersion.

Local Beach · Camel Rides · Lively 📍 Bamburi · ~12 min · Free
Mandhry Mosque & Old Harbour

One of the oldest mosques in East Africa, dating to 1570. Nearby, the Old Harbour is a working dhow port where traditional wooden boats have traded goods across the Indian Ocean for centuries.

History · Architecture 📍 Old Town · ~20 min · Free

Shopping From modern malls to traditional spice markets

City Mall Nyali

Mombasa's premier modern shopping mall with international and local brands, a large supermarket (Nakumatt), cinema, food court, pharmacy, and banking services. All under one roof.

Mall · Supermarket · Cinema 📍 Nyali · ~8 min
Kongowea Market

The largest fresh produce market on the North Coast. A sensory feast of tropical fruits, vegetables, fresh fish, and spices at the lowest prices in the city. Best visited in the morning.

Fresh Produce · Local Market 📍 Kongowea · ~10 min
Old Town Craft Market

Browse hand-carved wooden sculptures, Masai beadwork, Swahili ceramics, kikoi fabrics, and aromatic spices. Bargaining is expected and part of the fun. Great for unique souvenirs.

Souvenirs · Crafts · Spices 📍 Old Town · ~20 min
Nyali Cinemax & Shopping Centre

A relaxed neighbourhood shopping centre with a cinema, restaurants, and retail shops. Ideal for a casual evening out. Free parking available.

Retail · Entertainment 📍 Nyali · ~7 min

Getting Around Mombasa Safe and convenient transport options for all budgets

Bolt & Uber

Both apps operate widely in Mombasa and offer safe, metered rides at competitive prices. Highly recommended for reaching the city centre, beaches, and attractions without haggling.

Ride-Hailing · Safe · Metered
Tuk-Tuks

The iconic three-wheeled tuk-tuk is perfect for short trips within Nyali and coastal areas. Agree on the price before boarding — typical short trips cost KSh 100–300.

Local Transport · Affordable
Matatu (Minibus)

The local bus network covers most of Mombasa at very low cost (KSh 30–80 per journey). Ask our team for route advice. Best avoided during peak rush hours (7–9 AM, 5–7 PM).

Budget · Local
Private Driver

We can recommend trusted local drivers for full-day excursions, airport transfers, and multi-stop tours. A private driver for a full day costs approximately KSh 3,000–5,000.

Premium · Day Tours · Airport
Airport transfers: Moi International Airport (MBA) is approximately 30–40 minutes from our properties. We can arrange a trusted pickup — just let us know your arrival time when booking.

Guest Pro Tips From the Gonah Homes team — straight from local experience

  • Best time to visit beaches: Early morning (7–9 AM) and late afternoon (4–6 PM) offer the coolest temperatures and calmest waters. The midday heat (11 AM–3 PM) can be intense — use SPF 50+ sunscreen.
  • Bargaining is cultural: At markets and with tuk-tuk drivers, polite negotiation is normal and expected. A smile goes a long way — vendors respect guests who engage with the local culture.
  • Drinking water: Tap water in Mombasa is not recommended for drinking. We provide bottled water on arrival. Supermarkets sell 20L jerricans at budget prices for extended stays.
  • Dress code in Old Town: Out of respect for the predominantly Muslim Old Town community, visitors are encouraged to dress modestly (cover shoulders and knees). Lightweight scarves and wraps work perfectly in the coastal heat.
  • SIM cards & data: Purchase a Safaricom or Airtel Kenya SIM at the airport or any supermarket for excellent 4G coverage across Mombasa. Safaricom has the strongest signal at the coast.
  • Learn a few Swahili words: "Jambo" (Hello), "Asante" (Thank you), "Hakuna matata" (No problem). Locals genuinely appreciate any effort to speak Swahili — it will earn you warm smiles and often better prices!
